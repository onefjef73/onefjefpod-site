A Beer for Dad, Cards for Grandma

For my first Día de Muertos in Mexico City, Este helps me make an ofrenda. I put out a beer for my dad and a deck of cards for my grandmother. There’s also a parade, a giant skeleton and lots of tourists wearing terrifying makeup. Also, I drank the beer. Sorry, Dad.

The following weekend, I sat sweating in a temazcal in Tepoztlán screaming "forgive me".
