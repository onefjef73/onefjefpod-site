    Hitchhiking Across the USA with Jonathan Wiest

    I met Jonathan Wiest in a dysfunctional Spanish class in Mexico city, soon after he hitchhiked from the Texas border to Vancouver with $500 and a sign reading "north and west." We talk about what he found — conspiracy theories, roadside prayers, a truck named "Murdered," and a lot of friendly people stuck in an unfriendly country.

    Holy Mole, Oaxaca and a Tooth Being Yanked From My Face

    The joys of Oaxaca and the horrors of dental anxiety, with some foot fingers, excessive girth and a Rumi poem about teeth along the way.

  3. Episode 56Aug 20, 202630 min

    Why Are There Strings on Mexico City's Garbage Bags?

    I hire a Mexican lawyer to get my deposit back, visit a dentist I found on an expat forum, and spend an entire day trapped at home waiting for Amazon. Plus: I investigate the mysterious strings on Mexico City's garbage bags, contemplate the economics of the camote man, and have the strange realization that I'm happy here.

  4. Episode 55Aug 12, 20261 hr 14 min

    Biking the Entire Western Hemisphere with Gary Clarke (Re-Release)

    I met Gary in a Spanish class in Mexico City. I didn’t learn much Spanish, but I did make a friend who was riding a bicycle from Alaska to Patagonia. We talk about that, his old life on oil rigs, and eating guinea pig for breakfast.

  5. Episode 54Aug 5, 20261 hr 47 min

    The Strange Freedom of Living Elsewhere with Chris Ryan (Re-Release)

    Chris Ryan is the author of Sex at Dawn and host of Tangentially Speaking. We talk about living and traveling abroad, what happens when you get outside the American bubble, and how seeing other places can change the way you see your own country. We also get into my film Coverage, sex and death, 9/11, politics, Mexico, unemployment, podcasting, hitchhiking, and the unexpected connections that come from talking to people you might never otherwise meet.

    (This episode was originally released on 12/11/2025.)

  6. Episode 53Jul 29, 20261 hr 44 min

    Moving to Columbus Twice, Underground Yoga in Soviet Lithuania and Finding Somatic Therapy with Anastasija Katcyna (Re-Release)

    Anastasija grew up in post-Soviet Lithuania, got a green card at 18, and spent years bouncing between Columbus, London, Edinburgh, and Norway before eventually landing back in Ohio and becoming a somatic therapist and yoga teacher. We talk about growing up post-Soviet, the green card bureaucracy, and how a business trip to Columbus turned permanent once the pandemic hit.

    (This episode was originally released on 9/23/2025.)

  7. Episode 52Jul 22, 20261 hr 6 min

    Addiction, Guatemala & Whole Animal Butchery with Jason Wachtelhausen (Re-Release)

    Jason Wachtelhausen has lived one of the most unconventional lives of anyone I've interviewed. As I've been living in Mexico this year, I found myself thinking about his years in Guatemala and hearing this conversation a little differently. We talk heroin addiction, punk rock, butchery, religion, travel, and a life that refuses to fit into a box.

    (This episode was originally released on 6/17/2025.)

  8. BonusJul 16, 202626 min

    Uncomfortably Comfortable

    I settle into my new apartment (and terrace) in Mexico City while Jonathan's voice memos chronicle the final stretch of his hitchhiking journey across the United States. Plus: altitude flatulence, joy, and an announcement about the podcast.

  10. BonusJul 3, 202631 min

    The Friendly Empire

    I return to the United States for the first time since moving away and quickly remember what America does—and doesn’t—feel like. Jonathan continues hitchhiking toward Canada, collecting stories that range from deeply moving to completely bizarre: strangers who pray for him, unexpected generosity, Native ceremonies, truck stops, and conversations that reveal just how differently people experience the same country. Two very different journeys through America, happening at the same time.
