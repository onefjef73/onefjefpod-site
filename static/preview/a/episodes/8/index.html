  4. Episode 10Aug 5, 202549 min

    Wyoming, Devil’s Tower & Getting Lost in a Reno Casino (Road Trip Part 2)

    I continue the road trip from the wide-open emptiness of Wyoming to the neon chaos of Reno, Nevada. Along the way I visit Devil’s Tower, meet a cowboy named Sam, and discover just how disorienting a hotel parking garage can be at midnight.   5. Episode 9Jul 29, 20251 hr 2 min

    Gateway Arch, Mega-Crosses & My Sundance Origin Story (Road Trip Part 1)

    I kick off a cross-country road trip from Ohio to South Dakota, stopping at the Gateway Arch, passing enormous roadside crosses, and wondering what the hell American Christianity is doing with Jesus. Somewhere between the interstate and a tiny motel, the bottom literally falls off my car, and I tell the long, messy story of how my film ended up at Sundance. It’s rambling, roadside audio about travel, faith, breakdowns (mechanical and otherwise), and how a guy from Ohio wound up with a movie in Park City.

  6. Episode 8Jul 22, 20251 hr 34 min

    Combat Missions, PTSD & Life After War with Danny Eakins

    I talk with my friend Danny Eakins — a former Army Major who led more than 300 combat missions in Iraq and now works with Veterans Services helping soldiers and their families. We get into what combat actually does to a person, how PTSD shows up years later, the strange afterlife of military service, dating as a veteran, personal growth, and even Napster. It’s a grounded conversation about war, recovery, identity, and what it takes to rebuild a life once the adrenaline is gone.

  8. Episode 7Jul 15, 20251 hr 20 min

    Creativity, Discipline and Making Thousands of Songs on Spotify with Matt Farley

    I talk with Matt Farley — the wildly prolific musician behind Motern Media, known for recording thousands of songs on Spotify (including the infamous poop tracks). We get into how he built a creative life out of volume, discipline, and weirdness; the reality of DIY filmmaking; why niche art survives in a homogenized America; and what happens when you commit to your own strange corner of the world long enough for it to work. It’s a conversation about making things, finishing things, and not waiting for permission.

  9. Episode 6Jul 8, 20251 hr 47 min

    Sobriety, Microdosing & Finding Emotional Maturity with Andi Wesley

    I sit down with yoga teacher and energy worker Andi Wesley to talk about sobriety, family patterns, emotional maturity, and the weird space between being introverted and extroverted. We get into micro-dosing, boundaries, healing work, and what it takes to actually change your relationship with yourself. It’s a conversation about clarity, self-awareness, and the long, unglamorous work of growing up.
