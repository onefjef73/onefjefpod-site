About

The podcast

Expat life in Mexico City: interviews and solo dispatches about language, culture, and what it really feels like to leave your life behind and move to CDMX.

Jef Taylor

Jef Taylor is an editor, filmmaker, and reluctant grown-up. Since moving to Mexico City, he’s turned onefjef into a podcast about starting over in a new country. He talks to people (and sometimes himself) about learning Spanish, getting to know the city, and figuring out what makes a place feel like home. Before podcasting, he spent years shaping other people’s stories. Now he’s telling his own.
